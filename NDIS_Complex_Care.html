NDIS Complex Care in Victoria

7Aura provides NDIS Complex Care for participants with high support needs, dual diagnoses and complex behaviours. Our trained team delivers safe, professional and compassionate care in the setting that suits you, whether that’s at home, in Supported Independent Living (SIL) or through Short Term Accommodation (STA).

Some supports are described as too hard, or participants are told they are not suitable. We take the opposite view. We work with you, your family and your health professionals to build the right support environment around you.

As an NDIS-registered provider in Victoria, we focus on safety, dignity and confidence in everyday life. Get in touch to talk about your Complex Care needs.

What is NDIS Complex Care?

Complex Care refers to high-intensity disability supports that need skilled, competency-checked workers. These supports go beyond standard personal care and help participants with significant health needs live safely at home and in the community. They include:

  • Complex bowel care, catheter care and continence support
  • Enteral feeding, tracheostomy management and ventilation support
  • Seizure management and medication administration
  • Pressure injury prevention and complex psychosocial support

We follow your clinician’s instructions and your NDIS-approved plan, always putting dignity, consent and safety first, while you keep choice and control over your daily life.

High-Intensity Supports We Provide in Victoria

We provide Complex Care for NDIS participants who need skilled daily supports. Every plan is tailored to the individual and delivered in consultation with healthcare professionals, and we also support many other complex care requirements beyond those listed here.

Complex Bowel Care

Skilled support with bowel routines, careful monitoring and clear escalation steps, so you stay comfortable, healthy and in control of your day.

Enteral Feeding and Mealtime Support

Safe tube feeding (PEG, PEJ or NG) and mealtime support, delivered exactly as your dietitian and clinical team have set out.

Catheter and Continence Care

Hygienic, dignified catheter care and continence assistance from workers who are trained and competency checked.

Tracheostomy and Ventilation Support

Support with tracheostomy and ventilation needs, following your plan and the guidance of your clinicians.

Seizure Support

Support that follows your documented seizure management plan, with workers who know the warning signs and what to do.

Pressure Injury Prevention

Repositioning schedules, skin checks and the right equipment, to protect your skin and prevent pressure injuries before they start.

Medication Administration

Medication support delivered in line with your plan and our policies, with accurate records and prompt reporting of any concerns.

Psychosocial and Behaviour Support

Complex psychosocial support and day-to-day implementation of your behaviour support plan, in a calm, consistent and respectful way.

How We Work Together

Getting started with Complex Care should feel clear and manageable. Here is how we move from your first enquiry to supports that are safe, reliable and reviewed regularly. Your NDIS plan and your clinicians’ advice guide every step.

1. Intake and Needs Discussion

We start with a conversation about you, your health, your goals and what a good day looks like, so we understand what matters most.

2. In-Home Assessment

We visit to look at your routines, preferences, home set-up and any risks, so supports are planned around real life.

3. Care Plan and Staff Matching

Your care plan is co-designed with you, your family and your clinicians, and we match you with workers who have the right skills and the right fit.

4. Service Delivery and Documentation

Trained workers deliver your supports to the plan, with clear notes and handovers so everyone stays informed.

5. Review and Outcomes Reporting

We track outcomes and hold regular reviews, adjusting your supports as your needs, health and goals change.

Mapping Your Funding

We help you understand how Complex Care fits within your Core Supports, Assistance with Daily Life and any high-intensity budget lines, so supports line up with your plan.
