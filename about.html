About 7Aura

At 7Aura, we believe every person deserves to live with dignity, independence and a strong sense of belonging, whatever their ability. As an NDIS support provider based in Victoria, we deliver personalised, inclusive and respectful disability support that helps people live life on their own terms.

From Supported Independent Living and Short Term Accommodation to Complex Care, Support Coordination and Community Access, our services are shaped around you, your goals, choices and lifestyle, so support fits your life, not the other way around.

Our dedicated team works closely with participants, families and the wider community to design support plans that reflect each person’s goals, needs and aspirations. At 7Aura, you’re not just receiving a service; you’re gaining a team that listens, respects and walks alongside you every step of the way.

What we offer

Our Support Programs

At 7Aura, every program is flexible and led by you. We shape our NDIS supports around your circumstances, goals and routines, so you decide what help looks like and when it happens.

Supported Independent Living (SIL)

Shared homes with the right level of support, helping you build everyday skills, confidence and independence while staying connected to your community.

Individualised Living Options (ILO)

Living arrangements designed around you, so you can choose where, how and with whom you live, with support that adapts as your life changes.

Short Term Accommodation (STA)

Welcoming short stays that give you a change of scene, and your family and carers a well-earned break.

NDIS Complex Care

Skilled, attentive support for higher and more complex needs, delivered with respect so you can stay safe, well and in control.

Support Coordination

Practical guidance to understand your plan, connect with the right providers and make the most of your NDIS funding.

Community Access

Support to join the activities, outings and groups you enjoy, so you can build friendships and stay active in your community.

Every program is built on choice, independence and inclusion. At 7Aura, we’re more than a support provider: we’re a partner who walks beside you, helping you live the life you choose.

Frequently asked questions

Common Questions About NDIS Support

The National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) funds supports for people with permanent and significant disability. In general, you need to be an Australian citizen or hold an eligible visa, live in Australia, be under 65 when you apply, and have a permanent and significant disability, or meet the early intervention requirements. The NDIA assesses every application, so we always recommend checking the official NDIS website or speaking with our team if you are unsure.

Yes. Choice and control is at the heart of the NDIS. You decide who delivers your supports, and you can change providers at any time. Depending on how your plan is managed, you may be able to use both registered and unregistered providers. If you are not sure what that means for you, we’re happy to talk it through.

Start by checking your eligibility on the NDIS website, then complete an Access Request and provide evidence of your disability, such as reports from your doctor or allied health professionals. If you are found eligible, you will have a planning conversation to set your goals and funding. Our team can explain each step and help you get organised.

If your circumstances or needs have changed, you can ask the NDIA for a plan review, and you can also request a review of a decision about your plan. It helps to gather up-to-date reports and notes on how your needs have changed. We’re happy to help you prepare so you feel confident going into the conversation.
