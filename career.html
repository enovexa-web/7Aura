Join the 7Aura team in Victoria and help people with disability live the life they choose. We value kindness, reliability and people who genuinely care.

Working With 7Aura

7Aura is a registered NDIS provider based in Victoria. We are committed to delivering high-quality, person-centred support that puts the choices and goals of people with disability first.

As we grow, we are looking for dedicated and compassionate Support Workers to join our team. We offer flexible working hours, a supportive work environment and opportunities to pick up additional shifts, so you can build a rewarding career with room to grow.

If you are passionate about making a difference in the lives of people with disability, we would love to hear from you.

Join our team

Mandatory Requirements

To work with 7Aura, you will need the following. If you are missing any of them, get in touch and we can talk through the next steps.

  • NDIS Worker Screening Check (clearance)
  • NDIS Worker Orientation Module
  • Working with Children Check
  • CPR and First Aid Certificate
  • Current Driver’s Licence
  • National Criminal History CheckRequired in some circumstances
