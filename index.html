Registered NDIS Disability Support from 7AURA

7AURA provides NDIS disability support from Victoria, helping participants live with greater choice, comfort and independence. Our trained team delivers safe, respectful and compassionate care in a range of settings, including participants' homes, Supported Independent Living (SIL), Individualised Living Options (ILO) and Short Term Accommodation (STA).

We understand that every person's journey is different. That is why our approach focuses on personalised support plans that promote dignity, wellbeing and independence, built around your goals and delivered with reliability.

Specialist Complex Care Services

At 7AURA, we deliver specialised care for individuals with complex health and disability-related needs. Our approach prioritises safety, comfort and individual wellbeing, with support tailored to each participant's care requirements and daily routines.

Our complex care assistance includes:

  • Complex bowel care and ongoing monitoring
  • Enteral nutrition and feeding tube assistance (PEG/PEJ)
  • Catheter management and continence support
  • Tracheostomy care in accordance with clinical instructions
  • Seizure monitoring and response support
  • Pressure care, skin protection and regular repositioning
  • Medication assistance and administration as prescribed
  • Psychosocial support for complex needs
  • Behaviour support plan implementation

We also accommodate a range of specialised care needs through individualised support plans, working alongside participants, families and healthcare professionals to promote quality of life and continuity of care.
